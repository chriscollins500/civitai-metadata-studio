# A thoughtful place to build

Civitai Metadata Studio should be approachable to a first-time user and useful to a technical expert. We want direct evidence, honest disagreements, and room to learn without being belittled.

## Our standards

- Be respectful across experience levels, backgrounds, identities, accessibility needs, and creative interests.
- Critique the behavior, claim, or implementation—not the person. Explain evidence and tradeoffs rather than demanding agreement.
- Ask before sharing someone else's private material. Keep reproduction files minimal, relevant, lawful, and yours to share.
- Respect accessibility feedback and use descriptive text when a screenshot alone would be difficult to understand.
- Keep discussion on the project. Do not use support threads to advertise, spam, or recruit people into unrelated disputes.
- Correct mistakes openly. Distinguish a hypothesis, a tested observation, and a verified result.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, impersonation, and deliberate publication of private information are not acceptable. Good-faith disagreement, difficult bug reports, and discussion of supported media are not misconduct merely because they are uncomfortable or critical.

These standards apply to repository issues, pull requests, Discussions, reviews, and other spaces explicitly maintained for this project. GitHub's own platform rules also apply.

## Moderation

Maintainers may ask for a change, remove or edit inappropriate content, move an off-topic conversation, lock a thread, or restrict participation. The response should be proportionate to severity, recurrence, and safety/privacy impact. Serious threats or exposed personal information can require immediate action without a warning.

Moderation is not a promise that every report is proven, every disagreement will be resolved, or every decision will be publicly explained. Avoid discussing personal circumstances or reproducing abusive material in public. Maintainers are expected to follow the same standards.

## Reporting and review

For harassment, threats, doxxing, or other platform abuse, use GitHub's **Report content / Report abuse** flow. That channel goes to GitHub, not to a separate private inbox operated by this project. Do not post private details in an issue or Discussion to get attention.

For a non-sensitive moderation question, use [General Discussions](https://github.com/chriscollins500/civitai-metadata-studio/discussions/categories/general) and describe the concern without naming private individuals or repeating harmful content. Ask for review calmly; maintainers can reconsider a decision when relevant new context is available. There is no guaranteed response deadline.

For a software vulnerability, use the distinct [private security reporting channel](https://github.com/chriscollins500/civitai-metadata-studio/security/advisories/new). Do not use security advisories for ordinary conduct disputes or private support attachments.

## Keep participation easy

You do not need to be an expert, contribute code, or agree with a maintainer to belong here. Help us keep the conversation useful, privacy-conscious, and kind.
